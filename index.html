First, a quick safety check

New or worsening pain, numbness, diabetes / neuropathy, or a recent injury? A self-check cannot tell you what is safe for your body. Ask a qualified clinician before testing, changing shoes, or starting drills. Sudden weakness or inability to bear weight needs prompt medical care.
