Film four walk videos: front, back, left side, and right side. If one doesn’t track, you can replace just that clip.

The clearer your video, the more accurate your results. Good light and a full view from your shoulders down make the biggest difference.

  • Set up: phone sideways at hip height. Prop it up, or have someone hold it and stay in one spot. Barefoot, in shorts.
  • Front: start about 20 feet back and walk straight toward the phone. Stop about 6 feet away.
  • Back: start about 6 feet in front of the phone and walk straight away from it.
  • Sides: walk across the phone’s view, 10 to 12 feet away, with that side facing it.
  • Each clip: 6 to 8 natural steps. Start recording, then walk. No need to trim.
